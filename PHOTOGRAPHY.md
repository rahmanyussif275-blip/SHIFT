# Photography and product detail integration

The catalogue UI accepts real, product-specific photography paths through each product's `image` property and real try-on paths through `modelImages.slim`, `modelImages.average`, and `modelImages.plus` in `assets/js/products.js`. Use well-lit flat-lay or ghost-mannequin images for catalogue cards; never place an on-body image in `image`. Only use model images for the opt-in detail-page fit preview, with appropriate consent and exact garment/colour matching. Add real owned/licensed photo assets under `assets/products/` and point the product fields at their relative URLs.

No product photography or verified buyer review dataset was provided with the repository. The storefront therefore uses clearly marked garment illustrations, shows no fabricated model photos, and labels review status honestly. These are not realistic photographs. Replace placeholders with exact-item product photos before describing the catalogue as photo-complete. A missing model preview is clearly disclosed. Swatch colors update the illustrative garment; exact color-specific photos should be added for production.

Product data and listed GSM/price/category values are proposed catalogue content, not verified inventory specifications. Confirm with SHIFT before sale. Size controls S–XXL are presentation selections; confirm stock and sizing with the store.
